# AI Usage Policy for Interledger Contributors

## 1. AI Usage Is Accepted

Contributors are welcome to use AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Gemini, etc.) in their day-to-day work, for coding, writing, research, debugging, or planning.

## 2. Use AI, Don't Outsource Your Contribution to AI

The Interledger Foundation **allows the use of AI as a support tool** when contributing to the organization's GitHub repositories. Contributions include issues, pull requests, and comments. As a contributor using AI, you must disclose which tools you used and how you used them. For example, "I used Claude to generate and test the code samples".

You **may be asked to provide clarification or additional reasoning** for your contributions. If there's a lack of participation or if participation seems generic or off-topic, we may dismiss your contribution.

Be aware that **generative AI may not describe Interledger technologies accurately**. Similarly named ideas and the evolving nature of technology can result in conflated or outdated misrepresentations of concepts.

**Work with it, don’t make it work instead of you.** Never ship, submit, or share AI-generated output (code, analysis, documents) without reviewing and verifying it yourself first. Trust, but verify.

## 3. We Recommend Direct Communication To Be in Your Own Voice

When communicating directly within Interledger repositories, always use your own voice, not AI-generated text or what we like to call Work Slop.

AI can help you think through what you want to say, but the words you send to a community should be yours. Direct communication is how trust and working relationships are built. Humanity should still be the main focus in all things you do, so use AI only as a tool.
